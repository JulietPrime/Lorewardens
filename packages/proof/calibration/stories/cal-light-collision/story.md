Vesperhall went quiet an hour after sunset, as the curfew demanded.

The wardens were not inventing the old law: the Curfew Gate is the only gate in Vesperhall's north wall, and it has been sealed since the siege.

No light reached the harbor taverns that night; the night crew played dice by memory.

No light burned in any window of the harbor row, and the fog kept the shore dark.

In the morning the lamp trimmers went along the quay, refilling each oil box before the fog lifted.

The north wolves did not come down from the high hills that spring.

A pair of gulls argued over a fish head on the breakwater, loud against the hush.

By the time the market opened, the harbor master had already forgotten the quiet night.
