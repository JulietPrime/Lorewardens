Tamsin climbed the tidewright station stairs two at a time, late for the morning tally.

The harbormaster's clerk had written Tamsine on the duty roster, and no one had corrected the ink.

Rhonan was already on the pier, counting the keels against the manifest.

The tally sheet itself read Tamzin, in the tidy hand of the station master.

"I remembered the old channel marker," Rhonan said, not looking up.

Tamsin wondered whether the roster clerk even knew her by sight.

She signed the tally, corrected the clerk's spelling with two strokes, and handed the sheet back.

The tide came in around the pilings while the two of them worked through the manifest line by line.

By dusk the roster read clean, and the tidewright bell rang the first watch in.
